## Giga Pet – Sibil the cat

Sibil has four stats: weight, happiness (purrs per min), energy and name. Each of the four buttons changes them:

| Button   | Happiness | Weight | Energy |
|----------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Treat    | +10       | +3     | –      |
| Play     | +15       | -1     | -15    |
| Exercise | -10       | -3     | -15    |
| Sleep    | +5        | –      | +30    |

- Play and Exercise need at least 15 energy. With less, Sibil is too tired and nothing changes.
- Weight stays between 3 and 50 lb, happiness between 0 and 100, and energy between 0 and 100.
- Each button press shows a speech bubble with a comment from Sibil. Up to 5 bubbles stay on screen.
- Treat, Play and Sleep start purring when happiness is 50 or more. Higher happiness means faster purring. Purring stops below 50.
- The Mute button turns the purr sound on and off.
- Mood effects:
  - Happiness 70+: Sibil bounces.
  - Happiness 20 or less: Sibil turns gray and droops.
  - Weight 40+: Sibil gets wider.
  - Weight 8 or less: a dashed box appears around Weight.
